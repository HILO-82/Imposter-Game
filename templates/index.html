IMPOSTER

Social deduction word game

Aim

A secret word is given to all Crewmates. Imposters don't know it. Each round, players give one-word clues about the word, then vote to eliminate someone. The goal is to find the Imposter before they take over.

Crewmate

Knows the secret word. Gives clues to identify other crewmates without revealing the word to the imposter.

Imposter

Doesn't know the word. Blends in by watching clues and tries to avoid being voted out.

Jester

May get partial word info. Wins by getting voted out — act suspicious!

Game Flow

  1. One-word clue phase — each alive player gives a hint
  2. Vote phase — players vote to eliminate a suspected imposter
  3. If the Jester is voted out, the Jester wins
  4. If all Imposters are eliminated, Crewmates win
  5. If Imposters outnumber Crewmates, Imposters win
